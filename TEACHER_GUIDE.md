# City_tour — teacher’s guide

Mr Cole is a teacher in Russia. He is visiting his family in Australia. Lena is **a friend from Russia** and asks for a postcard. Emma is a teacher in Australia and invites Cole to help with a lesson about life in Russia.

## Suggested lesson

1. Use Free walk and place names to introduce the town. Practise next to, between, behind, in front of and opposite.
2. Switch to Missions. Read a message and discuss the destination before accepting it.
3. Choose New lesson, then Follow directions for the continuous eight-task walk. Keep place names off for a challenge.
4. Let partners give their own spoken directions in Free walk.

## Controls

The fixed start is in front of the bus shelter, facing **down ↓**, away from the shelter. Left and right always mean **Cole’s** left and right. Use ↑ to walk, ← / → to turn, ↓ to turn around. Turn first, then walk. Continue straight through intermediate pavement junctions when the message tells you to do so.

The garage, police station and post office are joined. Walk around an end of the whole row to reach the courtyard behind them. Never try to pass between these three buildings.

### Task 1: bus stop → post office

Start facing down ↓. Turn left. Go straight on to the corner. Turn left. Go straight on to the zebra crossing. Cross the road. Turn left. Go straight on to the first entrance. It’s on your right.

### Task 2: post office → garage

Start facing left ←. Go straight on. Go past the police station. Stop at the next entrance. It’s on your right.

### Task 3: garage → theatre

Start facing left ←. Go straight on to the corner. Turn right. Go past the garage. Turn right into the courtyard. Go straight on to the first entrance. It’s on your left.

### Task 4: theatre → café

Start facing right →. Go straight on. Go past the cinema. Stop at the next entrance. It’s on your left.

### Task 5: café → baker’s

Start facing right →. Go straight on to the zebra crossing. Cross the road. Go straight on to the first entrance. It’s on your left.

### Task 6: baker’s → library

Start facing right →. Go straight on to the corner. Turn right. Go straight on to the far corner. Turn right. Go straight on to the first entrance. It’s on your right.

### Task 7: library → post office

Start facing left ←. Go straight on to the zebra crossing. Cross the road. Go straight on to the first entrance. It’s on your right.

### Task 8: post office → school

Start facing left ←. Go straight on. Go past the police station and the garage. Turn right at the corner. Go straight on to the zebra crossing. Cross the road. Turn right. Go past the hospital. Cross the road at the next zebra crossing. Go past the park. Cross the road at the next zebra crossing. Go straight on to the first entrance. It’s on your left.

After task 8, Alex offers additional routes. Changing modes or reloading from another location can create a connecting task before the next story task.

## Road rules: can / can’t / must / mustn’t

Выберите режим Road rules. Нажмите на знак на карте. На телефоне указана роль Driver или Pedestrian и даны слова-подсказки. Ученик сначала произносит правило; Show examples открывает возможные ответы. Next sign и нажатие на другой знак снова скрывают образцы. Read aloud читает первый образец. В этом режиме Коул остаётся на своей позиции; дорожные задания не меняют сюжетные маршруты.

- Parking: You can park here.
- No parking: You can’t park here. / You mustn’t park here.
- Speed limit 30: You mustn’t drive faster than 30 km/h.
- No entry: You can’t drive into this area. / You mustn’t drive into this area.
- Ahead only: You must go straight on.
- Turn right: You must turn right.
- No right turn: You can’t turn right here. / You mustn’t turn right here.
- Pedestrian crossing: You can cross here when the pedestrian light is green. / You must look both ways before you cross.

Для синих стрелок требуется формулировка обязанности с must. У запрещающих знаков допустимы can’t и mustn’t. Число 30 обозначает максимальную скорость. Устные ответы не получают автоматическую оценку; обсуждайте другие верные формулировки.

Знаки связаны с конкретными участками: парковка с размеченными местами находится за больницей; жёлтый бордюр у гаража обозначает запрет парковки; столбики защищают пешеходную дорожку за школой. Turn right относится к машине, которая едет вниз вдоль больницы, Ahead only — к машине, которая едет вверх от банка, No right turn — к машине, которая едет вверх вдоль школы. Эти направления движения указаны на телефоне. Маленькой нумерации на знаках нет.
